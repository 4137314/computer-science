#+title: Carry-lookahead and prefix adders
#+summary: Unrolling \(c_{i+1}=g_i+p_ic_i\) shows that carries are a prefix computation with the associative operator \((g,p)\circ(g',p')=(g+pg',\,pp')\); computing group generates and propagates in a tree gives all carries in \(O(\log n)\) levels instead of \(n\).

* Construction
Unroll: \(c_2=g_1+p_1g_0+p_1p_0c_0\), and so on. For a group \([i{:}j]\),
\[ G_{i:j}=g_i+p_iG_{i-1:j},\qquad P_{i:j}=p_iP_{i-1:j},\qquad c_{i+1}=G_{i:0}+P_{i:0}c_0. \]
The operator \((G,P)\circ(G',P')=(G+PG',\,PP')\) is associative, so all prefixes \(G_{i:0}\) can be computed by a parallel-prefix tree ([[id:tree-height-reduction][tree height reduction]]):
- *4-bit CLA blocks* with a second level of lookahead: delay \(O(\log_4 n)\) levels;
- *Kogge–Stone*: \(\log_2 n\) levels, \(n\log_2n\) operators, fan-out 2, many wires;
- *Brent–Kung*: \(2\log_2 n-1\) levels, about \(2n\) operators.
Simpler alternatives: *carry-skip* (bypass a block when \(P=1\)) and *carry-select* (precompute both block sums), see [[id:generalized-bypass][bypass and select]].

Kogge–Stone for 8 bits: at level \(\ell\) each position combines with the one \(2^{\ell-1}\) to its right; three levels.

* Intuition
Instead of waiting for the carry to walk across the word, every block asks in parallel "would I produce a carry, and would I pass one on?", and the answers are combined like a tournament bracket.

* Pitfall
Logarithmic depth is paid in wiring: Kogge–Stone's long wires and fan-out make it large and power hungry, so real designs choose sparse trees or hybrid carry-select schemes.
