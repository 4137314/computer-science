#+title: Setup and hold constraints
#+summary: Data must be stable at a flip-flop's input from \(t_{su}\) before to \(t_h\) after the clock edge; between two registers this gives \(T\ge t_{cq}+t_{logic,max}+t_{su}\) (setup, fixes the clock period) and \(t_{cq}+t_{logic,min}\ge t_h\) (hold, independent of the clock).

* Statement
For a path from register \(R_1\) through combinational logic to register \(R_2\), with clock-to-output delay \(t_{cq}\), longest and shortest logic delays \(t_{max}\), \(t_{min}\), and skew \(t_{sk}\) (clock arriving later at \(R_2\)):
\[ T \;\ge\; t_{cq} + t_{max} + t_{su} - t_{sk}, \qquad t_{cq} + t_{min} \;\ge\; t_h + t_{sk}. \]
The maximum clock frequency is \(f_{max}=1/T_{min}\), set by the slowest register-to-register path.

* Derivation
A value launched by \(R_1\) at edge \(k\) leaves at \(t_{cq}\), crosses the logic in at most \(t_{max}\) and must arrive \(t_{su}\) before edge \(k+1\) at \(R_2\), which comes at \(T+t_{sk}\): \(t_{cq}+t_{max}+t_{su}\le T+t_{sk}\). The same edge \(k\) also makes \(R_2\) [[id:lambda-expressions][capture]] the *old* value; the new one, arriving at the earliest after \(t_{cq}+t_{min}\), must not corrupt it before \(t_h\) after the edge at \(R_2\): \(t_{cq}+t_{min}\ge t_{sk}+t_h\). \(T\) cancels from the second inequality.

The data must not change inside the window around the capturing edge.

* Fixing violations
- *Setup*: shorten the [[id:static-timing-analysis][critical path]] (logic restructuring, faster gates, [[id:retiming][retiming]], [[id:pipelining][pipelining]]) or lengthen the period.
- *Hold*: add delay (buffers) on the short path; lowering the frequency does not help.

* Intuition
Setup is a race the data must win against the next edge; hold is a race the data must not win against the current one.

* Pitfall
A violated constraint leaves the [[id:sequential-logic][flip-flop]] possibly metastable: its output can hover between 0 and 1 for an unbounded time. Asynchronous inputs, which can violate any constraint, need a two-flip-flop synchroniser.
